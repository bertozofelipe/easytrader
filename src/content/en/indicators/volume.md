**Volume** is the number of units of the asset traded in each period. It is not really a calculated indicator, but it is one of the most important pieces of information on the chart: it shows **how many participants took part** in a move.

## How to read it

- **Rally on rising volume:** many participants backing the rise; the move has conviction.
- **Rally on falling volume:** fewer people buying at each new price; the move may be losing strength.
- **Breakouts:** when price breaks a support or resistance on volume well above average, the breakout tends to be more reliable.
- **Volume spikes during sharp drops** can mark moments of panic and capitulation.

## Derived indicators

- **Volume average:** helps spot when the day's volume is above or below normal.
- **OBV (On-Balance Volume):** adds volume on up days and subtracts it on down days, to see whether volume confirms the trend.
- **VWAP:** volume-weighted average price, widely used in day trading.

## Caveats

- In crypto, the volume of a single exchange (such as Binance, used in Easytrader's chart) is only part of the total volume.
- In forex there is no centralized volume; platforms show their own broker's trade count.

On crypto charts, volume appears as bars at the bottom: green on up candles and red on down candles.
