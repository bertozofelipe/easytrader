A **candle** (or candlestick) sums up what happened to the price over a time interval — 1 minute, 1 hour, 1 day and so on.

- **Body:** the distance between the open and the close. Green/white when it closed above the open; red/black when it closed below.
- **Wicks (shadows):** show the period's high and low.

Candlestick patterns help read the balance between buyers and sellers.
