---
termo: Stop loss
resumo: An order that automatically closes a position when the price reaches a set loss level.
relacionados: [take-profit, risco-retorno]
---

A **stop loss** is an order placed together with (or right after) entering a trade. If the price moves against you and reaches the set level, the position is closed, capping the loss.

**Example:** you buy BTC at $60,000 and place the stop at $58,800. If the price drops there, the position is closed with a 2% loss.

In very volatile markets the stop may be filled at a worse price than the one you set (*slippage*).
