---
termo: Fear & Greed
resumo: A 0–100 index that estimates market sentiment, from extreme fear to extreme greed.
relacionados: [candle]
---

The **Fear & Greed index** sums up market participants' mood in a single number. In crypto, the most widely used version (alternative.me) combines volatility, volume, social media, Bitcoin dominance and internet searches.

- **0–24:** extreme fear
- **25–44:** fear
- **45–55:** neutral
- **56–75:** greed
- **76–100:** extreme greed

It shows **sentiment**, not where the price is going. Extreme readings tend to appear in moments of euphoria or panic, but they can last a long time. Use it as context, never as a standalone signal.
